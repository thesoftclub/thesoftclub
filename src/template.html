01 · Editor's Letter

“You don't have to carry the world all the time. There should be room for you to enjoy it, too.”

Life is more than working, achieving and getting through responsibilities. Softness is not about being passive or avoiding life's challenges. It is about giving yourself permission to live, connect, play and rest while you build the life you want.

That is why we started The Soft Club: a place to slow down, try something new and meet people worth knowing.

The Soft ClubFounding team
